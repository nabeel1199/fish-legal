Privacy Policy

Fish · Last updated 18 June 2026

Fish ("Fish", "we", "us") is a mobile application that helps adults meet travellers and locals, plan trips, and connect. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using Fish, you agree to this Policy.

The service is operated by Nabeel Khan. For any privacy request, contact us at nabeel.khan3009@gmail.com.

1. Who can use Fish

Fish is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data — email address, and (if you use Google or Apple sign-in) the name and email those providers share with us.
  • Profile data — display name, date of birth / age, photos, bio, gender identity, sexual orientation, who you're interested in, your intent (e.g. dating, friends, travel), travel vibes, and countries visited. Some of this is sensitive personal data (e.g. sexual orientation); you provide it voluntarily so we can offer the core matching features, and you can remove it at any time.
  • Location data — with your permission, your device's approximate/precise location, used to show nearby people, "Crossings", trip overlaps, and distance. You can turn this off in your device settings (some features will then be limited).
  • Content & activity — messages, postcards, ripples, likes, matches, trips, pods, stars, blocks, reports, and safety check-ins you create in the app.
  • Device & technical data — device type, app version, a push-notification token, and an advertising identifier used to serve ads.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and profile.
  • To power matching, discovery, location features, chat, and the app's other features.
  • To keep Fish safe — reviewing reports, blocking, and preventing abuse, fraud, and underage use.
  • To send you service and (if enabled) match/message notifications.
  • To show ads to non-paying users, and to process subscriptions for Fish Pro.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Where the law requires (e.g. in the EU/UK), our legal bases are your consent (for sensitive data, location, and marketing), performance of our contract with you, and our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4. What other users see

Your profile (name, age, photos, bio, identity you choose to show, intent, vibes, approximate distance, and city) is visible to other Fish users as part of discovery and matching. Messages and postcards are shared with the people you send them to. Please don't share information you wouldn't want others to see.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers — Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, messaging) and Google AdMob (ads). Sign-in is also handled by Google and Apple. Subscriptions are processed by Apple, Google Play, and our billing provider (RevenueCat).
  • Other users — as described in section 4.
  • Legal & safety — when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the public.

6. Advertising

Free users may see ads served by Google AdMob, which may use your advertising identifier. You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising; Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Subscribing to Fish Pro removes ads.

7. Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by emailing nabeel.khan3009@gmail.com from your account email, or by using in-app account deletion where available. Some data may be retained where required by law or for legitimate safety purposes (e.g. records of reports or bans).

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (including Google Firebase's security infrastructure and access rules) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process and store data on servers outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact nabeel.khan3009@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We'll revise the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, notify you in the app.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: nabeel.khan3009@gmail.com.
